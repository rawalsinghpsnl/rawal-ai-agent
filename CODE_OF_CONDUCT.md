# Code of Conduct — rawal-ai-agent

## Pledge

We pledge to make participation in `rawal-ai-agent` a harassment-free experience
for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender,
experience level, nationality, religion, or sexual identity.

## Standards

Positive: empathy, respectful feedback, constructive criticism, focus on what is
best for the community. Unacceptable: sexualized language, trolling, insults,
harassment, publishing others' private info, or any conduct inappropriate in a
professional setting.

## Enforcement

Violations may be reported to the maintainers via GitHub private report. All
complaints are reviewed confidentially. Maintainers may warn, temporarily ban,
or permanently ban contributors who behave inappropriately.

## Scope

Applies in all project spaces (repo, issues, PRs, discussions, chat) and in
public spaces when representing the project.
